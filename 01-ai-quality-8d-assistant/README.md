# AI Quality & 8D Assistant

## Business problem

Convert unstructured quality complaints into structured problem-solving artifacts.

## AI solution

AI-assisted extraction, 8D drafting, 5-Why hypotheses, corrective-action candidates and validation planning with human approval.

## Architecture

```mermaid
flowchart LR
A[User / Operational Input] --> B[AI Processing]
B --> C[Knowledge / Data]
C --> D[Recommendation]
D --> E[Human Validation]
E --> F[Business Action]
```

## Technology

`Python · pandas · 8D · 5-Why · RCA`

## Business value

Potential outcomes include reduced manual effort, faster response, better knowledge reuse and more consistent processes.
